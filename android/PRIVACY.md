# Soundalike for Android: privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Soundalike has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Nothing about you is sold or shared for tracking.

## What leaves your phone

- **The song you look up.** To find soundalikes, the app sends the song's title and artist and, when known, its Spotify track id, its ISRC or the song link you shared to `soundalike.yassin.app`. The request carries no identifier for you or your device.
- **Song details and previews** come from Deezer's public API (`api.deezer.com`), Spotify's public track pages, Apple's iTunes Search API and YouTube's public oEmbed endpoint. These requests contain song ids or names only. Cover images are loaded from the addresses those services give.
- **Opening a song** hands a link to the music app you chose (Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube Music), or to your browser when that app is not installed.

## Notification access

Android shows the playing song only to apps with notification access, so the one-tap features ask for it. Soundalike uses it for one thing: to read, from the media session of your music app, the playing song's title, artist and, where the player publishes it, its track id and cover address. It does not read, store or send the content of any notification, and it works without this access if you only share songs to it.

## Other optional permissions

- **Notifications**: only to show "Find soundalikes" while music plays, if you turn that on.
- **Display over other apps**: only to draw the floating button, if you turn that on.
- **Usage access**: only to know whether your music app is the app in front, so the floating button shows there and nowhere else. The app looks at the name of the foreground app and keeps nothing.

## What stays on your phone

Your settings, the songs you looked up (History, which you can clear) and Android's own system log of what the app did and how long it took. None of it is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes it.

## Children

Soundalike is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
