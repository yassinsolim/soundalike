Repeated-section genre & feeling study

This 20-seed Jamendo study compares a matched full-track audio control with one fixed genre, mood/theme, and instrument challenger. The recommender still ranks whole tracks; the player now presents one 20-second excerpt centered on the strongest repeated section so long intros and outros do not dominate your judgment. These excerpts are recurrence-based and are not verified chorus labels.

Seeds are fixed in an uncertainty-prioritized order: familiar core scenes come first, and lower agreement between the two anonymous top-five lists comes first within each group. Existing ratings never influence this order. Lists stay anonymously randomized per session.

Language is not tested on this page. Jamendo has no trustworthy track-language field. Spotify lyrics-language filtering remains a separate, unchanged production layer.

This exploratory study cannot promote a model or change production recommendations. No rating is submitted automatically. The UI shows attribution and licenses only after judgment; public license records remain inspectable. Method identities are absent from this page and public pack.
