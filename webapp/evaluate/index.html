Soundalike active listening study

Listen to one seed and four candidates. Pick the most similar, the least similar, and one primary reason the worst candidate misses.

Each player is limited to an approximately 20-second strongest-recurrence excerpt. This is a repeated-section heuristic, not a verified chorus label. Candidate ordering prioritizes disagreements that are most useful to resolve.

High-confidence vocal and singing-language mismatches are gated before this study; ambiguous tracks remain eligible. This research does not change production recommendations or promote a model.
