soundalike

any song · beta

Pick any song, even one we've never heard. We listen to its 30-second preview live and find songs that share its melody, its samples or its sound.

Searches all of Deezer. A song we haven't heard before takes a couple of seconds the first time.
Results come from a catalog of about 270,000 songs. Covers and shared melodies are matched most reliably. Samples are found when the sampled part is in both previews, and "sound" is a place to start exploring, not an answer. How we measured it.
Matching uses 30-second Deezer previews, so it only hears part of each song. ▶ = preview. Back to soundalike
